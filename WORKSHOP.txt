[h1]Performance Fixes[/h1]
Smoother shop, same game. Fixes stutters in the base game when you work with big, packed storage.

[h2]What's new in 1.3.1[/h2]
[list]
[*]A preventive fix: a locked container window now stays hidden, as in the base game. Nothing else changes.
[/list]
1.3.0 made loading a save much faster (8.3 s to 4.6 s on a real tested large store) and frees the store's memory when you leave it.

[h2]What it does[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Windows.[/b] Opening, closing and reopening full containers no longer stutters, and storage presets open without freezing animations.
[*][b]Inventories.[/b] Large inventories are laid out once per frame instead of once per change, and selecting or dragging many items stays smooth.
[*][b]Loading.[/b] Loading a save does far less repeated work, and leaving the store frees its memory.
[*][b]Customers.[/b] The buying check on nested storage runs once per item.
[/list]

[h2]What stays the same[/h2]
[list]
[*]Customers accept and refuse exactly what they did before.
[*]Windows look, stack and drag as before.
[*]Every fix can be switched off on its own in the mod's options (gear icon in the Mods menu).
[/list]

Needs no special permissions. Works on its own or with the other mods in the collection. Built for game version 0.46D.

[h1]性能修复[/h1]
店铺更流畅，玩法不变。修复原版游戏在处理大型、塞满的存储时的卡顿。

[h2]1.3.1 新内容[/h2]
[list]
[*]预防性修复：上锁的容器窗口现在和原版一样保持关闭。其他内容不变。
[/list]
1.3.0 大幅加快了读档（在实测的大型店铺上从 8.3 秒缩短到 4.6 秒），并会在离开商店时释放内存。

[h2]功能[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]窗口。[/b]开关和重新打开满载容器不再卡顿，存储预设打开时动画不会停住。
[*][b]库存。[/b]大型库存每帧只布局一次，而不是每次变化都布局；选中和拖动大批物品保持流畅。
[*][b]读档。[/b]读档时少做大量重复工作，离开商店时释放内存。
[*][b]顾客。[/b]嵌套存储的收货判定对每件物品只执行一次。
[/list]

[h2]保持不变的[/h2]
[list]
[*]顾客收什么、拒什么与原来完全一致。
[*]窗口的外观、叠放和拖动与原来一样。
[*]每项修复都可以在模组选项（模组菜单中的齿轮图标）里单独关闭。
[/list]

不需要任何特殊权限。可单独使用，也可与合集里的其他模组一起使用。适用于游戏版本 0.46D。
