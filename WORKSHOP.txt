[h1]⚠ Windows from other mods[/h1]
With Hide closed windows instantly on, a closed container window is kept switched on but not drawn, instead of being switched off. A mod that checks whether its window is switched on, opens it with SetActive(true) or refreshes it in OnEnable may then fail to open it again, or open it with old contents. If a window from another mod misbehaves, turn that option off in this mod's settings.
[h1]⚠ 其他模组的窗口[/h1]
开启「瞬间隐藏关闭的窗口」后，关闭的容器窗口不会被停用，只是不再绘制。如果其他模组检查窗口是否启用、用 SetActive(true) 打开窗口，或在 OnEnable 里刷新窗口，它的窗口可能打不开，或打开后仍是旧内容。遇到这种情况，请在本模组的设置里关闭这个选项。

[h1]Performance Fixes[/h1]
Fixes stutters in the base game when you work with big, packed storage.

[h2]What's new in 1.3.1[/h2]
[list]
[*]A preventive fix: a locked container window now stays hidden, as in the base game. Nothing else changes.
[/list]
1.3.0 made loading a save faster: on a store of 1,342 items, from the click to the first frame, 8.3 s became 4.5 s. It also frees the store's memory when you leave it.

[h2]What it does[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Windows.[/b] Opening, closing and reopening full containers no longer stutters, and storage presets open without freezing animations.
[*][b]Inventories.[/b] Large inventories are laid out once per frame instead of once per change, and selecting or dragging many items stays smooth.
[*][b]Loading.[/b] Loading a save does far less repeated work, and leaving the store frees its memory.
[*][b]Customers.[/b] The buying check on nested storage runs once per item.
[/list]

[h2]What stays the same[/h2]
[list]
[*]Customers accept and refuse exactly what they did before.
[*]Windows look, stack and drag as before.
[*]Every fix can be switched off on its own in the mod's options (gear icon in the Mods menu).
[/list]
